Open Wayframe

A photo board for the whole world

Pin your photos to the map. String them into a story.

Hang every photo where it was taken, then connect the stops with red string, the way a detective works a case, except the case is your trip.

Start your board

Free. Works in your browser. Your photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Three moves

  1. Pin

    Add photos and put each one where it was taken. Search any city, or click the map. Photos with GPS land by themselves.

  2. String

    Click photos in the order you went. Red string joins them into a route you can reorder, rename, and play back.

  3. AS

    Share

    Follow friends and their stories show up on your map in blue string. Nothing is public until you say so.

Built for cameras with no GPS

The Cyber-shot DSC-V1 never knew where it was. Wayframe does.

Old compact cameras write the date and the settings into every file, but not the place. Wayframe reads what’s there, shows it as a “shot on” label, and makes putting the rest on the map quick: drop a whole day of photos on one city in a click.

A map we draw ourselves

No map service. Countries, states, provinces, cities and US counties, drawn from public data that ships with the app, so it works offline too.

Play your trip

Press play and the map flies stop to stop while the string draws itself. Make a poster of any story to print.

Yours first

Everything works without an account. Sign in with email only when you want your photos on every device, or friends.

Stories are better together

See where your friends have been.

Pick a username, search for a friend’s, and follow them. Their profile shows who they are, and the stories they choose to share appear on your map beside yours, in blue.

  • Nothing is shared by default, photo by photo or a whole story at a time
  • Profiles show only a name, a username and a bio. Photos need a follow
  • Photos live in a private bucket behind expiring links
