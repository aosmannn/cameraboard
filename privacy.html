Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Wayframe is built so your photos stay yours. This page explains what we store, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Using Wayframe without an account

You can use the whole map without signing in. In that case your photos, stories and settings are saved only in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your email address, used to send you a sign-in code and to identify your account.
  • Your profile: a display name, an optional username, and your choices about being found and about the community gallery.
  • Your photos and their details: the image (resized), its title, your notes, the place and coordinates you set or the photo carried, the date, the camera and settings, the story it belongs to, and who can see it.
  • Your activity: who you follow and who follows you, likes, people you have blocked, and reports you send.
  • Contact matching: if you use “find friends by email”, we compare one-way hashes of the addresses. We do not store your address book.

Who can see your photos

Every photo and story is Private until you change it.

  • Private: only you.
  • Friends: people who follow you.
  • Public: anyone who has your link, including people without an account.

Public photos are listed in the community gallery only if you also turn on “Show my public photos in the community gallery” in your profile. You can turn that off at any time, and the photos are removed from the gallery right away. Anyone who can see a photo can see the place and details attached to it, so check the location before you make something public.

Your username is shown only if you choose to be discoverable. Images are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your data or show advertising.
  • We do not use your photos to train models.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking scripts on the site.

Services that help us run Wayframe

Wayframe relies on a few providers to operate: Supabase stores accounts, photos and profile data; Vercel hosts the website; and Google delivers our sign-in emails. They process data only to provide those services.

Your choices

  • Change who can see any photo or story at any time, or remove it. Removing a photo deletes its image from our storage.
  • Block or report someone from their profile or a photo.
  • Export a backup of your board from the menu in the map.
  • To delete your account and everything attached to it, email us and we will do it.

Children

Wayframe is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users.
