Place names

Place names and city information include data from GeoNames, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

Terrain map

On the Terrain theme, streets, rivers, lakes and buildings are drawn from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), delivered by OpenFreeMap in the OpenMapTiles format. Land heights come from © Mapterhorn, which combines the Copernicus DEM with open national elevation surveys. Seabed depth comes from the Mapzen terrain tiles on AWS Open Data, which combine SRTM, GEBCO, ETOPO1 and other public elevation sources. Climate and vegetation zones come from Natural Earth (public domain).
