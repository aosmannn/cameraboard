Place names

Place names and city information include data from GeoNames, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

The map

Country shapes, states, lakes, rivers, deserts, mountain ranges and peaks come from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. The climate-zone grid that colors forests, grassland, desert, tundra and ice was made from Natural Earth II land cover by Steven (@vcanp). All of it ships with Wayframe, so the map needs no outside map service.
